# 🎛️ Bank El Haz — Control Room

> A host's dashboard for keeping every game in check.

One place to **watch and manage all the games** — current, finished, and empty — and step in whenever you need to.

---

## 👀 See everything at a glance
- **Live games, finished games, lobbies, and empty rooms** — all in one view.
- Each room shows who's playing, who's online, the host 👑, whose turn it is ▶, and the winner 🏆.
- **Search** by room or player name, **filter** by status, and **sort** however you like.

---

## 🔍 Peek inside a room
Open any room to see:
- The **players**, their **cash**, **positions**, and **status**.
- The **properties** they own (with houses, hotels, and mortgages).
- The room's **activity log** (who did what).

---

## 🛠️ Manage a game
- **Edit players** — rename them, change their cash, move them around the board, eliminate or revive them, turn them into bots (or back to humans), or remove them.
- **Restart** a game — send everyone back to the lobby with a fresh board (players stay).
- **Control the flow** — set whose turn it is, change the phase, declare or clear a winner, or skip to the next turn.
- **Add bots** to a room.

---

## 🗂️ Keep things tidy
- **Delete** a single room.
- **Clear all rooms** (live, finished, lobbies, and empty) for a clean slate.
- **Clean empty rooms** in one click.
- **Copy a room's invite link** to share it.
- **Download a room's data** for the record.

---

## 💡 Handy to know
- Sign in with your **admin account** to get started.
- Changes apply **instantly** to the players in that room.
- Anything destructive (**restart / delete / clear all**) asks you to **confirm** first.
- It updates on its own, and it won't jump around while you're reading a room.
